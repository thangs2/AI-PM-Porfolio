01 — PMBOK® Guide 8 mapping (Focus Areas, domains, outcomes)

Purpose

Map this pack onto PMBOK Guide — Eighth Edition (Nov 2025, ANSI/PMI 99-001-2025) — not PMBOK 6 process groups alone, and not PMBOK 7’s eight domains as the live grid.

PMBOK 8 has three layers. Focus Areas are only the when. Outcomes live in each domain’s Check Results: did the intended result happen, not “did we run a process.”

Seat: TDM. A JD may still say TPM. Same seat.

This is a tailored hybrid / adaptive application (Standard §4.2–4.3, Guide §3 Tailoring). OpAnalysts is ongoing pod work. Cadence is sprint/Kanban pull, not a single predictive close.

How PMBOK 8 is read here

PMBOK 7 leftover (why the earlier line sounded better): Delivery / Planning / Project Work / Measurement / Uncertainty were domains. In 8 they moved: Delivery → Scope (+ quality in Governance); Planning → Focus Area, not a domain; Project Work → Governance; Measurement → Monitor and Control … inside each domain; Uncertainty → Risk + principle Adopt a Holistic View.

1. Six principles → pack law

Mindset (PMBOK 8): Proactive (holistic + quality) · Ownership (accountable + empowered) · Value-driven (value + sustainability).

Principle Pack behavior
Adopt a Holistic View SoT conflict, SoW vs backlog, CS vs Fraud vs Pay, deps, incident leftover — one system, not local tickets
Focus on Value Outcome AC, not Kafka-as-AC. DEFER / CHANGE / TRADEOFF instead of silent insert. Pods pull Ready
Embed Quality Into Processes and Deliverables A–G + hierarchy. Auto-Ready only under policy. DONE_FAIL if Done has no evidence. uncited-claim never auto
Be an Accountable Leader TDM owns policy, sample, exceptions, honest status — not every Ready click, not merge, not how
Integrate Sustainability Thin and honest: PII / secret / prod-write / irreversible — legal and blast-radius, not a full ESG program
Build an Empowered Culture Sender owns red. Pod owns pull and how. Lead owns trade-off. AI does not rank departments

2. Value delivery (Standard §2.1) — output is not outcome

PMBOK 8 still distinguishes output → outcome → benefit → value. Amateur PM reports outputs (tickets Done). This pack checks outcomes.

Level Meaning Pack Check Result
Output Artifact produced Classified JSON · /verified slice · Ready card · status pack draft
Outcome Usable change in how work runs Pod can pull without TDM mailbox. Red returns to sender. Exceptions are a minority after Phase 1
Benefit Measurable improvement Intake→Ready down without bounce-up. Hallucinated AC to pod = zero. Auto-Ready overturn rate tracked
Value Why the org hired a TDM 5–6 pods moving (Q07). Status Head can use. Commercial gaps are Lead decisions, not pod fights

Success metrics already in 01 are the benefit row — they are Check Results, not vanity velocity.

9. Interview line

PMBOK 8 is principles + seven domains with Check Results + five Focus Areas. This pack is the TDM value-delivery system for five to six pods. Governance is exception-based auto-Ready. Scope is A–G and evidence of Done. Schedule is WIP, spike clocks, and honest Blocked. Stakeholders send red back to the sender. Risk is quarantine and incident — not a Ready story. Finance here is unfunded/SoW, not EVM. Closing is increment evidence. We tailor: adaptive cadence, not a waterfall close.
